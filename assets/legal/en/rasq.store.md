SNSs and other services connected to through the use of a Plugin are also subject to the conditions of those services.

Purchasing one does not start an agreement for the other. If the same right of use has already been acquired by one-time purchase, period fees for the same scope are not charged in duplicate. Fees for additional features are displayed separately and applications for them are accepted separately.

### Article 3. Permissions and Handling of Data

1. At installation, the features the Plugin uses, the data it accesses, its communication destinations, and the purposes of use are displayed. The Plugin is not enabled until the user reviews this information and confirms the installation.
2. Data sent to an external service is also handled by that recipient. Consenting to RASQ alone does not mean consenting to unrelated purposes of use by third parties. If there are additional purposes, an explanation is given and any necessary consent is requested before execution.
3. Disabling or deleting a Plugin does not necessarily delete data already passed to a recipient automatically. The retention period, deletion method, and contact for inquiries can be checked in that Plugin's explanation of data handling.
4. Providing diagnostic information about Plugin communications is optional. The items provided, the recipient, and the retention period are displayed on a dedicated screen, and the information is sent only if the user enables it. Declining does not change the normal conditions of use of the Plugin.
5. For Plugins in which multiple users use the same screen (shared sessions), the Plugin's state and the values written by participants are stored on the RASQ public server and distributed to participants. Values you write are also visible to other participants. The retention period is set out in the Privacy Policy.

### Article 4. Updates

1. Users can choose from the methods provided: manual updates, updates that are acquired automatically, and updates that are acquired and used automatically. Depending on the method, the update may only be saved, or the active version may be switched.
2. Updates that expand permissions, communication destinations, accessible data, or purposes of data use beyond what the user last permitted are not enabled automatically. The added items are shown and confirmation is requested again. If the differences or the distribution cannot be verified, the update is put on hold.
3. You may decline an update. However, if the old version poses a serious risk or the specifications of a connection destination change, it may not be possible to continue using the old version. In that case, the reason, impact, and alternatives are explained. No new fee arises solely because an update was declined.

3. When a problem is confirmed, the Operator carries out investigation, warnings, updates, suspension of listing, and other responses. This article does not exempt the Operator from any contractual or legal liability it bears.

### Article 5-2. Plugins Not Obtained Through the Store

1. Users can install Plugin files without going through the Store (sideloading). Plugins that have not been reviewed run in "restricted mode" and cannot use external communication, some storage areas, or app operations such as opening the post screen, sharing to ASTell, or opening URLs.
2. Plugins signed with a developer key you have registered yourself, and Plugins bundled with the app, run without restrictions.
3. Unreviewed Plugins distributed on Swing or profile pages also run in restricted mode. It is indicated that they are unreviewed Plugins.
4. For Plugins that have not been reviewed, the Operator has not performed the confirmation through review and signatures under these conditions. Check the distributor before installing.

1. Where there is a specific reason, such as rights infringement, a risk of a serious information leak, false review information, violation of law, a legitimate request from a connection destination, or the end of provision, the Operator may issue warnings, exclude a product from search results, stop new acquisition and re-acquisition, or restrict the use of a specific version. In an emergency, restrictions are applied first, and the reason and how to respond are notified afterwards.
2. Suspension of listing alone does not automatically extinguish a license lawfully acquired by a user or rights based on third-party licenses. However, features that require re-acquisition, server features, and features that depend on external services may become unavailable due to suspension of distribution. Execution of a version posing a serious risk may be stopped.
3. The Operator limits the scope of suspension to what is necessary and, where possible, provides guidance on fixed versions, alternative versions, and how to export data. ### Article 2. Distinction Between One-Time Purchases and Subscriptions

1. Features and versions included in a one-time purchase and additional features and services provided by subscription are displayed separately on the product screen. Recurring fees are not added later to features already purchased on a one-time basis. Users are not made to purchase or renew the same right of use in duplicate.

1. Even if a Plugin is deleted, the purchase history of one-time purchase products remains. The person who owns the account used for the purchase can re-acquire the same purchased scope on supported devices. However, re-acquisition may not be possible due to suspension of distribution under Part A, Article 6 of this document, the end of a connection destination, or similar reasons. 2. Purchase rights cannot be transferred to an account different from the one used for the purchase, unless the product or license provides otherwise. Rights to copy and redistribute permitted by third-party licenses are treated separately from the purchase rights on this account.
3. Even if a period-based paid feature stops, rights to use other features or products already purchased on a one-time basis and free features are not lost. Which features stop is notified for each product.

### Article 4. Settings and Created Data After Stopping

1. A user's settings and created data saved on the device are not deleted immediately solely because a subscription ends or distribution is suspended. Whether viewing and export are possible after stopping, and the retention period and deletion timing of data on servers under separate agreements, are displayed on the product screen and in the notice of stopping.
2. Where possible, the Operator provides guidance on how to export data or on a corresponding alternative version. If a dangerous version cannot be run for safety reasons, the Operator explains whether there is a method of exporting data that can be used without running that version. Handling upon account deletion is governed by the "Pricing and Points Terms" and the Privacy Policy.
